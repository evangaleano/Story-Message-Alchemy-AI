A Message I Went Through Hell to Deliver

In 2018, I started my video business with -$126 in my bank account, sweating through a Ross dress shirt at local networking events. That same year, my autistic brother fell through our front window during an outburst, and I learned my parents might have to sell the house. I had one thing: a 7-minute documentary about our family that had not been seen because we were too private to share it. We posted it anyway. A week and 300 shares later, our community had raised a year of living expenses for my family to get back on their feet. Today they still live in my brother and I's childhood home, and as I walk up to the house these days tears still often well up in my eyes.

By mid 2019, I was hitting my first $30k+ months in business by repeating that same strategy: helping businesses and nonprofits capture their stories with an authentic, mini-documentary approach. I went from shooting website videos that boosted SEO and helped save brick-and-mortar businesses in my hometown to directing brand films for a $2B info-product company, then consulting on brand narrative for startups that went on to raise over $9 million in funding from story-driven launches and pitch decks. A few years later, I got on stage for a 30 minute keynote at a mansion mastermind, overcoming my fear of public speaking, and walked away with $37k in new business deals in a single day using the same frameworks I am giving you in this book.

After a hell of a journey, I discovered that within every leader's story lies a uniquely powerful message that only they can tell. AI cannot replicate it, and competitors cannot touch it.

In this book, I will reveal how to clarify and articulate that message in a way that communicates the power of the work you do and the one-of-a-kind leader you are.
